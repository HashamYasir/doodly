About DOODLY

A small corner of the internet for small games.

What is this?

DOODLY is a personal arcade. Every game here is original, made by one person, and plays instantly in your browser. There is nothing to download or install, and you do not need an account.

How it works

  • Pick a game and press Play.
  • Each game runs in its own sandboxed frame, so one game can never break another (or the site).
  • Some games remember your best score. That lives in your own browser and never leaves your device.
  • There are no ads and no tracking on this site.
